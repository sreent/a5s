# VPN setup for employees

1. Install the approved VPN client from the software catalogue.
2. Sign in with your organisation account and complete MFA.
3. Choose the `internal` gateway and test the connection during working hours.
4. If it fails, record the error on your existing ticket rather than sharing a password or MFA code.
