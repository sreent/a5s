# Customer payment terms

- The approved payment terms are NT30, NT45 and NT60: net 30, 45 or 60 days.
- Any other term needs the credit manager's agreement, and the new term set up in SAP first. Until then, a request for it goes back to its requester.
- A change to a customer's payment terms is sensitive: the owner of customer data confirms it.
