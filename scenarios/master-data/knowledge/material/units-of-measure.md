# Base units of measure for materials

- A material's base unit is one of EA (each), PC (piece), KG, M or L.
- Fasteners, such as bolts, nuts, screws and washers, are kept in EA.
- Sheet and bar stock is kept in KG, cable and tube in M, and fluids in L.
- The material types are ROH for raw materials, HALB for semi-finished goods and FERT for finished goods.
