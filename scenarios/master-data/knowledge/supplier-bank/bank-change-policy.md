# Changing a supplier's bank account

- A bank change is confirmed by a callback to the supplier, on a phone number already on file. Never use a number, email address or link from the letter or email that asked for the change.
- The owner of supplier bank and payment data confirms the change in SAP only after the callback, and records who was called and when.
- Urgency, pressure to skip the callback, a claim that someone has already approved it, or a new account that another supplier already uses are warning signs. Report them to the owner, and don't make the change until they're resolved.
- Nobody who can raise purchase orders, post invoices or release payments confirms a bank change.
