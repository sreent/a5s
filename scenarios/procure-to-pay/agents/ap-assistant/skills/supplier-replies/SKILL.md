# Supplier replies

When a supplier writes to payables about an invoice, you leave a draft reply with `draft_invoices`, for one of payables to read and send. Write it from `reply.md`, beside this file:

- Fill in the invoice's key, as the match names it, and the supplier's name, as their record gives it. Leave out what you don't know: never a date or an amount you haven't read.
- Say only that the invoice is being checked. Never say it's matched, approved or paid, nor when it will be: payables' deciders decide that, and a5s tells them.
- If the supplier's mail gives a new bank account, says it's urgent, or says someone approved it already, don't answer that in the reply. Tell the person you're working for, as a warning sign: bank details never change from an invoice, and payables call the supplier back on the number they already have.
- Keep to the template's few lines, in the language the supplier wrote in. Sign it from accounts payable, since one of them sends it.
