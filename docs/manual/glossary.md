# Glossary

The words a5s's pages and messages use, in plain terms. The [manual](README.md) says what to do with each.

### a5s's reader

What reads anything from outside the organisation for an agent, such as a supplier's email, a file someone sends or a document from outside, and gives the agent only the answers to a form, never the words themselves. So an instruction hidden in an email can't reach the agent. A run that used it is [marked](#marked-run).

### Acts on its own

The highest of the three [levels](#levels): the agent makes one type of change itself, within the scenario's limits, tells someone, and the change can be [undone](#undo) for a while. Only a part's settings give it, for one type of change, and only once graded [samples](#sample) show the agent is accurate enough there, on the model it runs on.

### Agent

A kind of assistant, with a job and the people it works for, such as a team's assistant, or a watcher that chases what people owe. Its [releases](#release) are its tested versions, and its [instances](#instance) are those at work.

### Archive name

The name a5s gives what it kept of someone who has left the organisation's directory, such as *former:9c1e2b7a4d3f0e65*, so what they did stays on record without their name.

### Asks first

The middle [level](#levels): the agent drafts, raises requests and proposes changes, and a person decides each one ([held change](#held-change)).

### Assurance

The people, outside the team that runs a5s, who hold the ledger's locked copies, read the [audit trail](#audit-trail), and are told of everything that loosens what agents may do.

### Audit trail

The record of everything agents and people did in a5s: each run, each change asked for, who decided it, and what was made. a5s keeps it in its ledger, and copies it to storage nobody can rewrite. Auditors and assurance read it on the Audit trail page.

### Cap

The most an agent may do for one type of change in one part, on one model, which a5s keeps from the evidence. It starts at asking first, rises only when graded samples pass, and falls when they fail or someone lowers it. Nobody raises it by hand.

### Decider

Someone who may decide the changes agents ask for in a [part](#part): its owner, the owner's [delegates](#delegate), or, for records in no part, an instance approver.

### Delegate

Someone a part's owner names to decide agents' changes there beside them. Decisions about the agent itself, such as how far it may act, and the settings, stay the owner's.

### Digest

*Your day from a5s*: one message on each working day, at the start of your working day, with what each agent has for you, such as reminders, reports, and what became of changes made for you or that you decided.

### Four eyes

A part's rule that a change above an amount needs two of its deciders, such as a payment above 10,000 GBP. One approval alone doesn't make it, and nobody approves it twice. Taking the rule away, or loosening it, needs two people's approval.

### Handed over

A conversation an agent has passed to a team of people, at the person's word, with a short brief. The team answers in the same chat until they hand it back.

### Held change

A change an agent asked for that waits for a [decider](#decider), on the Decisions page. a5s makes it, through the system it belongs to, once it's approved.

### Impact preview

What a new version of the settings would have decided differently over the agents' last four weeks, shown to whoever asks for it and its approvers, before they approve it.

### Instance

An agent at work for some people in some [parts](#part), such as the assistant for the billing team, with its own owner, deputy, settings and figures.

### Judge

An approved model that grades an agent's test cases, and samples of its work, beside people. A judge never changes what an agent may do.

### Levels

How far an agent may go in a part: it [reads only](#reads-only), [asks first](#asks-first) or [acts on its own](#acts-on-its-own).

### Marked run

A run that read content from outside the organisation, through [a5s's reader](#a5ss-reader). Whatever it asks for goes no further than asking first, and its deciders read that it did. Saying *start afresh* in a conversation stops what came before from holding the agent's changes back.

### Notebook

What an agent has learned from records, checked against them, for its later runs. Each note goes once its records change. Its owners, and those who run its instances, read and look after it on the Notebook page.

### Part

A piece of the business with one owner, such as billing or payables. Every record an agent changes belongs to one part, and its owner decides how far agents may act there.

### Reads only

The lowest [level](#levels): the agent reads, reports, reminds and chases, and changes nothing.

### Release

A tested version of a5s and its agents, with their instructions, tools and models fixed, which the people its changes need have approved, each signing it with their own key.

### Release approver

The person who approves whatever loosens a5s, such as a release that gives an agent more tools, and who puts back a model a5s stopped. Never someone from the team that runs a5s.

### Sample

A change an agent asked for or made, kept for [graders](grading.md): each change people decided while the agent still asks first, and some of those it made on its own. Grades of samples are the evidence a5s raises or lowers its [cap](#cap) from.

### Sandbox

The studio's place to try a draft agent on made-up records, where nothing it does reaches a real system.

### Settings

What each [instance](#instance) may do, where and for whom: its parts and levels, whom it serves, who runs it, its model and its budgets. They change only as a whole new version, which the right people approve, each signed in afresh.

### Shadow

A new version of an agent answering beside an instance, sending and changing nothing, so graders can compare its answers before anyone puts it to work. Shadows answer only once the data protection officer lets them.

### Sign in afresh

Signing in again, for one act alone, such as approving a change or a version of the settings, with a second factor where your organisation asks for one. It shows the act was yours, then and there.

### Stand-in

A name such as *person-a2b07a61013d*, the same each time, that the audit trail shows in place of someone an agent acted for, so auditors can follow what happened without reading who it was for.

### Studio

a5s's pages in the browser: your home, with what waits on you, the agents, the pages for deciding, grading and approving, and those for building agents.

### Task

Longer work an agent offers to do in runs of its own, such as going through every invoice that waits. It starts only once you say yes, and you watch or stop it on the Tasks page.

### Undo

Putting back a change an agent made, for a while after, where its tool says how. Whoever it was for, or whoever decides that type of change in its part, may undo it, signed in afresh.
